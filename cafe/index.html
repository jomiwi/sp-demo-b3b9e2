FAQ

Café questions

When is the café open?

D' Rafael's Cafe is open during court bookings, so it's there while you play. Our menu includes hot and iced coffee, frappes, fruity soda pop, fries, streetfoods, nachos and calamares.

What are your hours?

Court: 6 AM to 2 AM, every day. Café: open during court bookings. See Rates and hours.

Can we bring outside food?

Sorry, no outside food or drinks. D' Rafael's Cafe is right inside and opens when the court is booked.

Can we book the court and café for a private event?

Yes. Book the court for your group and message us about food and drinks.

How much is open play?

See each session's fee on Reclub. Every session is for all levels.
