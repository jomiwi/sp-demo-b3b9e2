Open play

Open play is how most people start here. You sign up for a session, show up, and we rotate everyone into games. You don't need a partner or a group.

  3. Pay by GCash. The first to pay gets the slot.

  4. Post your proof of payment in the session's Game Chat.

  5. Come a little early.

New to pickleball?

First time? You're welcome here

Pickleball is played with a paddle and a light plastic ball on a small court, so it's easy on beginners. Kids, parents and older players all play here, and nobody expects you to be good on day one.

  • No partner needed. Come solo and we'll rotate you into games.
  • No experience needed. Every open play session is for all levels.
  • No paddle? Rent one here for ₱100 per session.
Find an all-levels session Ask us a question

Your first visit

  1. Request to join an all-levels session on Reclub. Once you're on the waitlist, pay by GCash and post your proof in the Game Chat.

  2. Wear comfortable clothes and rubber-soled sports shoes. Bring water and a small towel. Paddle: bring your own, or rent one here for ₱100 per session.

  3. Come a few minutes early and tell the organizer it's your first time. They'll help you find a game.

  4. Take breaks whenever you like. Stay after for a coffee at D' Rafael's Cafe.

About the court

A court with a café, not just a court

We're a single pickleball court in Caloocan with our own café, D' Rafael's Cafe, inside Saranay Homes in North Caloocan. It's the first pickleball court in Saranay, and we built it as a place to stay a while.

Play a few games, then sit down for coffee or a plate of fishball and kwek-kwek. Bring your friends and family, from the kids to the grandparents. Players of every level share this court, and 586 of them have joined our club on Reclub.

There's a dartboard too, for when you're waiting for the next game.

FAQ

Questions we get

Quick answers, so you don't have to wait for a reply.

Do I need to be good to join open play?

No. All our open play sessions are for all levels. If it's your first time, tell the organizer and they'll help you find a game.

How do I join open play?

Join our club on Reclub, request to join a session and wait until you're on the waitlist. Then pay by GCash (the first to pay gets the slot) and post your proof of payment in the session's Game Chat. Need to cancel? Tell us at least 24 hours before. There's no cash refund, but you can transfer your slot or keep it as credit.

How much is open play?

₱150 per player for a 4-hour session. On coffee open play sessions, buying a drink at D' Rafael's Cafe gets you in free. coffee session days and times

How do I rent the whole court?

Book it on Sports360. It's ₱300 per hour from 6 AM to 10 AM, ₱200 from 10 AM to 4 PM, and ₱300 from 4 PM to 2 AM. Coming as a big group or planning an event? Message us on Facebook or Instagram, or call +63 906 545 8384, and we'll sort it out.

Can I rent a paddle?

Yes. Paddles are ₱100 per session. We also sell tournament-grade balls for ₱100.

Can we hold a party or private event here?

Yes. We've hosted private events on the court and in the café. Message us with your date and headcount and we'll work out the court time and the food.

What are your hours?

Court: 6 AM to 2 AM, every day. Café: café hours. See Rates and hours.

How many players per open play session?

Up to 12 players, on our one court. Slots go to whoever pays first once they're on the waitlist.

How do I pay?

Open play: through Reclub, by GCash. Court rental: you pay in full on Sports360 when you book, and bookings are non-refundable. Need to move it? Ask at least 24 hours ahead. Café: cash, GCash or both at the counter?

What should I bring or wear?

Comfortable clothes, rubber-soled sports shoes, water and a small towel. Paddles: bring your own, or rent one here for ₱100.

Is there parking?

parking details. For a Grab or tricycle, use the Copy location pin button.

Can I come just for the café?

Yes. You don't have to play. Sit, eat and watch the games. Our menu includes coffee, the Tusok-Tusok Treat (fishball, kwek-kwek, kikiam) and enoki crispy fries. café hours

Can we bring outside food?

Sorry, no outside food or drinks. Our café is right inside, so you won't go hungry.

Are kids welcome?

Yes. Kids and families play here. any age rule or supervision rule
