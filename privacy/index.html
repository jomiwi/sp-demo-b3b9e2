Your choice

We only use analytics with your consent. When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept. Until you answer, and if you tap Decline, none of our analytics tools load and nothing is sent to Google or Microsoft by them.

If you tap Accept, we load Google Tag Manager, which then loads Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity. We switch on analytics only. Google's advertising settings (ad storage, ad user data and ad personalization) stay off.

Your answer is saved in your browser's storage on this device (as saranay_consent), so we don't ask on every page. It never leaves your device. To change it, use the button below or at the top of this page. To start fresh, clear this site's data in your browser.

If you change from Accept to Decline, the tools stop loading on your next page view. Cookies they already set stay in your browser until they expire or you clear them.

The tools we use

Google Tag Manager

Google · container GTM-TZ4KN34J

A loader. It doesn't measure anything itself. After you accept, it loads the two tools below. Until then, it isn't loaded at all.

Google Analytics 4

Google · measurement ID G-Q9F8HPKEND

Counts visits and clicks, such as taps on "Book a court" or "Join open play", so we know which pages help players book.

Microsoft Clarity

Microsoft · project zcfsm7p371 · Strict masking

Records how visitors move around our pages (clicks, taps and scrolls) and builds heatmaps, so we can see what's confusing. In Strict mode, the words on the page and anything you type are hidden before anything is sent. A recording is a replay of page layout and movements, not a video of you or your screen.

What's collected

Only after you accept, these tools collect:

  • Pages you view and the page you came from.
  • Clicks, taps and scrolls, including which buttons and links you use.
  • Device and browser: for example phone or laptop, operating system, browser, screen size and language.
  • Approximate location: city or country, estimated from your IP address, which every web request carries. Google says Google Analytics 4 doesn't log or store IP addresses.
  • Random IDs stored in cookies, so visits from the same browser can be grouped. These IDs don't contain your name.

These tools don't collect your name, phone number, email address or payment details. We don't take bookings or payments on this site: bookings happen on Reclub and Sports360, under their own privacy policies.

What happens even if you decline

A few things aren't analytics, so they don't wait for your choice. We want you to know about them too:

  • Our web host. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs every visitor's IP address for security, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • The map on our home page. It's a Google Maps embed. When you scroll to it, your browser loads it from Google, which receives your IP address and browser details under Google's Privacy Policy. We don't receive that data.
  • Links to other sites. "Book a court" sends you to Sports360, "Join open play" to Reclub, and "Message us" to Facebook. Once you're there, their privacy policies apply.

Who receives the data

  • Google receives Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager data.
  • Microsoft receives Microsoft Clarity data and stores it in its Azure cloud. Microsoft says it acts as a data controller for Clarity data, under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Outside the Philippines. Google and Microsoft process data in the United States and other countries, so your data leaves the Philippines when you accept. We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone else.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the right to:

  • Be informed Know that your data is being collected, and how it's used. This page is part of that.
  • Object Say no to the processing. Tapping Decline does this, or you can contact us.
  • Access Ask what data we hold about you.
  • Rectification Ask us to correct data that's wrong.
  • Erasure or blocking Ask us to delete or block your data.
  • Data portability Get a copy of your data in a common electronic format.
  • Damages Be compensated if you're harmed by inaccurate, false or unlawfully obtained data, or unauthorized use of it.
  • File a complaint Complain to the National Privacy Commission.

Because these tools use random IDs instead of names, we may need the ID from your browser's cookies to find your data. Clearing your cookies and declining stops new data from being linked to you. Data already collected is deleted on the schedule above.
