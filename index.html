About the court

A court with a café, not just a court

We're a single pickleball court in Caloocan with our own café, D' Rafael's Cafe, inside Saranay Homes in North Caloocan. It's the first pickleball court in Saranay, and we built it as a place to stay a while.

Play a few games, then sit down for coffee or a plate of fishball and kwek-kwek. Bring the barkada, the family or the kids. Players of every level share this court, and more than 580 of them have joined our club on Reclub.

There's a dartboard too, for when you're waiting for the next game.

Open play

Open play

Open play is how most people start here. You sign up for a session, show up, and we rotate everyone into games. You don't need a partner or a group.

How it works

  1. Open our club on Reclub and pick a session.

  2. Request a spot. Reclub shows how to pay to confirm it (we use GCash). If the session is full, you go on the waitlist.

  3. Come a few minutes early, say hi at the counter, and get on the court.

New to pickleball?

First time? You're welcome here

Pickleball is played with a paddle and a light plastic ball on a small court, so it's easy on beginners. Kids, parents and older players all play here, and nobody expects you to be good on day one.

  1. Pick an all-levels session

    The easiest way in is an open play session marked "all levels". Tell the organizer it's your first time and they'll help you find a game.

Do I need to be good to join open play?

No. All our open play sessions are for all levels. If it's your first time, tell the organizer and they'll help you find a game.

How do I join open play?

Join our club on Reclub, pick a session and request a spot. You pay to confirm, and if it's full you go on the waitlist.
