Open play

Open play is how most people start here. You sign up for a session, show up, and we rotate everyone into games. You don't need a partner or a group.

How it works

  1. Request to join the session on Reclub.

  3. Pay by GCash. The first to pay gets the slot.

  4. Post your proof of payment in the session's Game Chat.

  5. Come a little early.

New to pickleball?

First time? You're welcome here

Pickleball is played with a paddle and a light plastic ball on a small court, so it's easy on beginners. Kids, parents and older players all play here, and nobody expects you to be good on day one.

  • No partner needed. Come solo and we'll rotate you into games.
  • No experience needed. Every open play session is for all levels.
  • No paddle? Rent one here for ₱100 per session.
Find an all-levels session Ask us a question

Your first visit

  1. Request to join an all-levels session on Reclub. Once you're on the waitlist, pay by GCash and post your proof in the Game Chat.

  2. Wear comfortable clothes and rubber-soled sports shoes. Bring water and a small towel. Paddle: bring your own, or rent one here for ₱100 per session.

  3. Come a few minutes early and tell the organizer it's your first time. They'll help you find a game.

  4. Take breaks whenever you like.

About the court

A court with a café, not just a court

We're a single pickleball court in Caloocan with our own café, D' Rafael's Cafe, inside Saranay Homes in North Caloocan. We built it as a place to stay a while.

Book the court, and D' Rafael's Cafe opens while you play: coffee or a plate of fishball and kwek-kwek. Bring your friends and family, from the kids to the grandparents. Players of every level share this court, and nearly 600 of them have joined our club on Reclub.

There's a dartboard too, for when you're waiting for the next game.

FAQ

Questions we get

Quick answers, so you don't have to wait for a reply.

Do you offer lessons?

Yes. Saranay offers lessons for new players, no experience needed. Message us and we'll send the next available slot and the price.

Do I need to be good to join open play?

No. All our open play sessions are for all levels. If it's your first time, tell the organizer and they'll help you find a game.

How do I join open play?

Join our club on Reclub, request to join a session and wait until you're on the waitlist. Then pay by GCash (the first to pay gets the slot) and post your proof of payment in the session's Game Chat. Need to cancel? Tell us at least 24 hours before. There's no cash refund, but you can transfer your slot or keep it as credit.

How much is open play?

See each session's fee on Reclub. Every session is for all levels.

How do I rent the whole court?

Book a court online and pick your time. It's ₱300 per hour from 6 AM to 10 AM, ₱200 from 10 AM to 4 PM, and ₱300 from 4 PM to 2 AM. Coming as a big group or planning an event? Message us on Facebook or Instagram, or call +63 906 545 8384, and we'll sort it out.

Can I rent a paddle?

Yes. Paddles are ₱100 per session. We also sell tournament-grade balls for ₱100.

Can we book the court and café for a private event?

Yes. Book the court for your group and message us about food and drinks.

What are your hours?

Court: 6 AM to 2 AM, every day. Café: open during court bookings. See Rates and hours.

How many players per open play session?

Up to 12 players, on our one court. Slots go to whoever pays first once they're on the waitlist.

How do I pay?

Open play: through Reclub, by GCash. Court rental: you pay in full on Sports360 when you book, and bookings are non-refundable. Need to move it? Ask at least 24 hours ahead.

What should I bring or wear?

Comfortable clothes, rubber-soled sports shoes, water and a small towel. Paddles: bring your own, or rent one here for ₱100.

When is the café open?

D' Rafael's Cafe is open during court bookings, so it's there while you play. Our menu includes coffee, the Tusok-Tusok Treat (fishball, kwek-kwek, kikiam) and enoki crispy fries. See the café menu.

Can we bring outside food?

Sorry, no outside food or drinks. D' Rafael's Cafe is right inside and opens when the court is booked.

Are kids welcome?

Yes. Kids, parents and grandparents all play here.

Still have a question? Message us and we'll get back to you.

Privacy and cookies

We don't take bookings or payments on this site. If you choose Accept, we use Google Analytics, a Google service loaded through Google Tag Manager, to count visits and clicks to Reclub, Sports360 and Facebook, so we know what helps players book. Google Analytics then sets its cookies (_ga) on this device and sends that usage data to Google. We don't turn on Google's advertising features: ad storage, ad user data and ad personalization stay off. If you choose Decline, or until you choose, neither Google Tag Manager nor Google Analytics is loaded and nothing is sent to Google. Your choice is saved on this device and you can change it any time.
